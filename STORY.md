# The Last Delivery — playable chapter one

## Premise
You are a night courier in a fictional Lagos-inspired district. Your partner Nia has stopped answering her phone while following a parcel addressed to a cemetery. Her last message asks you to find Mama T before the night bus leaves. You arrive with her shovel and an unfinished promise.

## Playable story beats
1. **The last message — market:** Find Mama T near the produce stalls and press T. Her dialogue identifies Palmline Logistics as the next lead. Accepting the lead changes the objective and map marker.
2. **The dispatch record — business quarter:** Reach the terminal outside Palmline Logistics and press T. The record names Remembrance Gardens, plot 1. The story marker moves to the distant cemetery.
3. **The cemetery parcel — Remembrance Gardens:** Excavate plot 1 from the edge. Inside a keepsake box is a blue-wrapped parcel and a note from Nia. She is safe and waiting at Bus Stop 04.
4. **Before the night bus — roadside district:** Cross the road and interact near the shelter. Nia gives back the small letters the player wrote her. The chapter ends with a reunion; the player can continue exploring or restart.

The cemetery location is a fictional hiding place for a keepsake parcel. This is a romantic mystery, not a depiction of real Lagos events. Company names, characters and plot are fictional. The main route does not require violence. Sandbox attacks, pursuit, dragging and burial remain available alongside it.

## Recovery and consequences
If Mama T is unavailable, a receipt at the stall preserves the lead. If plot 1 is excavated before the dispatch record, its discovery is remembered and recognized when that lead is accepted. If Nia is killed after appearing at the shelter, the ending uses a note rather than pretending she can speak. Nearby people flee attacks and conversations stop. There is no police/wanted system yet.

## Dialogue and routines
Market shoppers approach vendors and exchange short question/answer lines, with a small parcel-handoff animation. Workers move around office frontage; commuters wait at the stop; the caretaker remains near the cemetery gate. T opens dialogue when close. Optional browser speech reads text aloud; it is not actor-recorded dialogue and voice availability varies by system.
